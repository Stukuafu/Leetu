About RetroCart

Built by someone who still has the pins-cleaning brush.

RetroCart started as a way to make sense of a growing shelf of Mega Drive carts — knowing exactly what condition each one is in, what's actually in the box, and being straight about it before anyone asks.

Every listing here has been tested. If a cart is cart-only, we say so. If the label's faded or the case has a crack, that's in the notes — not left for you to discover after asking.

The catalog starts with Sega Mega Drive, since that's where the collection began, and will expand to other retro consoles as it grows. No account, no checkout yet — just a straightforward way to browse what's available and get in touch.

How condition grading works

MintAs-new, no visible wear, complete with all original materials.
ExcellentLight handling wear only, fully tested and working.
GoodNormal wear for its age — label fading, minor scuffs. Fully working.
FairNoticeable wear, may be missing extras. Tested and working unless noted.
PoorHeavy wear or cosmetic damage. Function confirmed and noted honestly.

RetroCart is an independent collector project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Sega.
